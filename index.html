What this is: every dot is a real Asian power station tracked by Climate TRACE (satellite-estimated emissions, CC BY 4.0). Why: before lending to or insuring an asset, you need its climate-risk shape. What to do: click any dot — or press Screen any point and click anywhere — and the panel on the right builds a live risk profile from 80+ years of measured weather, river records since 1984, and climate-model projections to 2050.

Coverage: coal is authoritative — every Asian coal plant in GEM's Global Coal Plant Tracker (Jul 2026) is shown; solid black = operating/mothballed, hollow black = pipeline (announced → under construction). 96% of operating coal plants also carry a Climate TRACE satellite emissions estimate. The clean layer is fully GEM-sourced and current — solar & wind (Feb 2026 releases), hydro & geothermal (Mar 2026), bioenergy (GBPT V3) and nuclear (Aug 2026), operating plants ≥50 MW (nuclear: all sizes), so the post-2020 renewables build-out is included. Non-power assets and plants below each tracker's threshold are not shown.

This is a point screen of the plant's immediate surroundings, not a map of the wider region.
